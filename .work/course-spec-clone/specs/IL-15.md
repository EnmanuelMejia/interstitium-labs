## Learning Outcomes (objectives)
1. Write advanced SQL fluently: window functions, CTEs, and analytics patterns.
2. Design batch and streaming pipelines with idempotency and data-quality gates.
3. Apply statistics and probability correctly: distributions, intervals, tests, Bayes.
4. Design and read A/B tests honestly: power, MDE, peeking, and multiple testing.
5. Analyze data with pandas: groupby, merges, missing data, and vectorized features.
6. Reason about ML interviews: theory, deep learning, coding, and ML system design.
7. Tell the story: visualization, metrics, and business judgment for analyst loops.
8. Model warehouses: star/snowflake, grain, slowly changing dimensions, and dbt.
