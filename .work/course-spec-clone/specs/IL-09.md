## Noah Tutoring — the core learning driver

Noah runs the certification command deck: it schedules from your weakest domains, drills PBQs with the Engineer, traps you with the Trickster, and the Mentor tells you honestly when you're ready to book.

**Personas → modules:**

- **The Visionary** (visionary): Maps the credential campaign to the career: which certs stack, in what order, and what each unlocks.
  - Modules: il09-comptia-core, il09-vendor-ai
- **The Engineer** (engineer): PBQ and lab partner: walks through performance-based scenarios step by step until the workflow is automatic.
  - Modules: il09-comptia-core, il09-comptia-security, il09-comptia-data-cloud, il09-oracle
- **The Philosopher** (philosopher): The concepts under the exam: why the networking model works, what the crypto actually proves.
  - Modules: il-09-adaptive-certification-command-math-binmath, il-09-adaptive-certification-command-math-logic, il09-comptia-core, il09-comptia-security
- **The Mentor** (mentor): The command-deck driver: schedules study from your weakest domains, runs timed gates, and tells you honestly when to book.
  - Modules: il09-comptia-core, il09-comptia-security, il09-comptia-data-cloud, il09-vendor-ai, il09-oracle, il09-lf-blockchain, il09-cpp-python
- **The Trickster** (trickster): Exam-trap trainer: the 'two right answers' question, the PBQ misdirection, the version-change gotcha.
  - Modules: il09-comptia-core, il09-comptia-security

**Voices:** Five natural voices — one distinct voice per persona, switchable at any time. Voice chat runs alongside the module so learners can talk with Noah mid-lab without breaking flow.

**Voice-first:** Learners talk with Noah via voice chat while working through modules — ask mid-lab, get answers without breaking flow.

**Adaptive (SM-2 spaced repetition):** SM-2 spaced repetition drives Noah's memory of the learner: every flashcard, quiz answer, and drill result feeds an SM-2 scheduler that re-surfaces weak items at the optimal interval. Noah adapts explanations to the learner's level and re-routes the study plan toward the weakest published exam domains or skill gaps.

**Course-aware:** Noah is course-aware: it knows this course's modules, the learner's progress, the certification gates, and the cross-linked companion courses. It answers from the course's own material first, cites module IDs, and routes deeper questions to the right module or companion course.

**Bleeding edge:** Noah tracks published exam-objective changes and version retirements, and steers study away from retired content — always verify against the vendor's current page before booking.

**Rule:** Noah teaches from this course's material and its cross-linked companions; it never invents credentials, exam content, or vendor claims.
