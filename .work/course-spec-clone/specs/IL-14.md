## Learning Outcomes (objectives)
1. Treat reliability as an interview discipline: SLIs, SLOs, error budgets, and incident command.
2. Design CI/CD pipelines and infrastructure-as-code with real rollback plans.
3. Reason about cloud architecture: networking, data services, and security controls.
4. Think like an attacker and interview like a defender across app, network, and crypto.
5. Rehearse full interview loops: DSA speed reps, system-design reps, and behavioral stories.
6. Narrate troubleshooting aloud under time pressure with a repeatable method.
7. Score yourself against interview rubrics and triage weak spots after every mock.
