## Learning Outcomes (objectives)
1. Operate a zero-trust endpoint defense platform at operator depth: allowlisting, ringfencing, elevation control, storage control, network control, Detect triage, and centralized Defender configuration.
2. Triage blocked actions like a Cyber Hero: read the denied event, classify severity, investigate the process tree, disposition every alert, and escalate with evidence.
3. Run break-fix under default-deny: diagnose hash-mismatch and policy breaks, scope the impact, fix through the exception workflow, and close the loop with process changes.
4. Execute the incident response lifecycle (NIST 800-61): scope, contain, verify, preserve evidence, escalate, and write post-incident reports with hardening actions.
5. Apply MIT 6.858 computer-security concepts — threat models, control hijacking, privilege separation, sandboxing, web security, TLS, side channels, authentication, privacy, security economics — to endpoint defense decisions.
6. Build and evaluate ML anomaly detectors and use AI triage copilots safely: AI proposes, the human disposes, and every AI claim is verified against primary evidence.
7. Use the prereq math spine (logic, boolean/bitwise, number theory, probability, linear algebra) to reason about policies, crypto, and detection statistics including Bayes' theorem for alert triage.
8. Prepare independently for CompTIA Security+ SY0-701, CySA+ CS0-003, ISC2 CISSP, GIAC GSEC, the MIT xPRO Professional Certificate in Cybersecurity, and the Google/IBM cybersecurity certificates — aligned to their publicly published objectives.
9. Map adversary behavior to MITRE ATT&CK tactics and explain 'why we blocked this' to technical and non-technical stakeholders.
10. Walk into the ThreatLocker IT Infrastructure Specialist panel interview with operator-grade technical depth, STAR behavioral stories from real history, and a same-day execution plan.
