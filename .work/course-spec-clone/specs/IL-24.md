## Learning Outcomes (objectives)
1. Describe ThreatLocker's zero-trust endpoint approach from public materials, in your own words.
2. Answer behavioral questions with operator-grade STAR stories.
3. Work default-deny triage: approve, deny, or ringfence with documented reasoning.
4. Explain allowlisting, ringfencing, elevation control, storage control, and network control operations.
5. Read endpoint posture: services, egress, file identity.
6. Think in attack graphs: nodes, edges, paths — and defend from the graph.
7. Explain how ML models and UEBA contribute to threat defense, honestly.
8. Reason about prompt injection and tool poisoning for AI agents.
9. Work an AI-aided SOC workflow: lifecycle, detection engineering, governance.
10. Apply Bayes and honest metrics to security claims; prove readiness under time in the simulation circuit.

## Noah AI Integration (required)

> Noah AI is the core learning-experience driver for this course — not a bolt-on. Every module is taught with Noah as the Socratic tutor: learners work through the material in conversation with Noah, which is course-aware (grounded in this course's modules, notes, labs, quizzes, and drills), adapts with SM-2 spaced repetition, and speaks with 5 natural voices for a voice-first experience. Learners can talk with Noah via voice chat while working through modules — asking questions, working problems aloud, and getting challenged back before answers are given.

**Driver:** Noah drives the learning experience: guided Socratic walkthroughs of each module, Socratic questioning before answers, drill generation from the learner's weak spots, and lab debriefs that connect hands-on work back to the module's objectives.

**Personas → modules:**

- **Visionary** — Shows the operator you are becoming: the Cyber Hero mindset — default-deny as a discipline, not a product. _(modules: m24-company-cold, m24-star-operator)_
- **Engineer** — Drills the controls: allowlisting, ringfencing, elevation, storage, network — exact, procedural, no shortcuts. _(modules: m24-default-deny, m24-approve-deny-ringfence, m24-endpoint-posture)_
- **Philosopher** — Asks why the attacker thinks the way they do: nodes, edges, paths — the graph view of malice. _(modules: m24-attack-graphs, m24-graph-defense)_
- **Mentor** — Runs the assessment simulation with you: STAR stories, triage logic, interview-day game plan. _(modules: m24-star-operator, m24-assessment-sim)_
- **Trickster** — Is the attacker in the simulation: crafts the phishing lure, the malicious macro, the bypass attempt — and grades your response. _(modules: m24-approve-deny-ringfence, m24-ai-aided-soc, m24-capstone)_

**Voices:** 5 natural voices. Five natural voices. Voice-first: learners talk with Noah via voice chat while working through modules — hands free for labs, terminals, and notebooks.

**Adaptive:** SM-2 spaced repetition. Noah schedules flashcard and drill reviews with the SM-2 algorithm, prioritizing cards and modules the learner gets wrong. Forgetting curves are fought per-learner, not per-cohort.

**Course-aware:** Noah tutors from the course itself — every explanation, hint, and drill references the module content the learner is on. No generic answers; everything is grounded in this course.
