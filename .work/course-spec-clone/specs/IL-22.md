## Learning Outcomes (objectives)
1. Distinguish primary texts from later receptions across the Western esoteric traditions.
2. Explain Kabbalah's core structures and its historical development within Jewish mysticism.
3. Read the Hermetic corpus with attention to its late-antique context.
4. Interpret alchemical symbolism historically rather than anachronistically.
5. Describe astrology's framework and tarot's history as cultural systems.
6. Evaluate Enochian, grimoire, and ceremonial-magic claims against manuscript evidence.
7. Compare Rosicrucianism, Theosophy, and the Golden Dawn as modern movements.
8. Apply source criticism: date, language, and manuscript tradition before interpretation.
9. Discuss Eastern esoteric traditions with comparative care.
10. Read contemporary occult revivals critically — enthusiasm with evidence.

## Noah AI Integration (required)

> Noah AI is the core learning-experience driver for this course — not a bolt-on. Every module is taught with Noah as the Socratic tutor: learners work through the material in conversation with Noah, which is course-aware (grounded in this course's modules, notes, labs, quizzes, and drills), adapts with SM-2 spaced repetition, and speaks with 5 natural voices for a voice-first experience. Learners can talk with Noah via voice chat while working through modules — asking questions, working problems aloud, and getting challenged back before answers are given.

**Driver:** Noah drives the learning experience: guided Socratic walkthroughs of each module, Socratic questioning before answers, drill generation from the learner's weak spots, and lab debriefs that connect hands-on work back to the module's objectives.

**Personas → modules:**

- **Visionary** — Traces the long arc: how hermetic ideas echo through centuries into modern culture and technology. _(modules: m22-hermeticism, m22-modern-revival)_
- **Engineer** — Demands source criticism: dates, manuscripts, languages — the philology behind the mystique. _(modules: m22-grimoires, m22-enochian, m22-kabbalah)_
- **Philosopher** — Explores the ideas on their own terms: what the traditions claim about reality, mind, and symbol. _(modules: m22-hermeticism, m22-gnosticism, m22-symbolism)_
- **Mentor** — Teaches critical reading: how to approach esoteric texts with both openness and rigor. _(modules: m22-approaching, m22-modern-revival)_
- **Trickster** — Tests your credulity: presents the sensational claim, then reveals the manuscript facts. _(modules: m22-grimoires, m22-divination, m22-modern-revival)_

**Voices:** 5 natural voices. Five natural voices. Voice-first: learners talk with Noah via voice chat while working through modules — hands free for labs, terminals, and notebooks.

**Adaptive:** SM-2 spaced repetition. Noah schedules flashcard and drill reviews with the SM-2 algorithm, prioritizing cards and modules the learner gets wrong. Forgetting curves are fought per-learner, not per-cohort.

**Course-aware:** Noah tutors from the course itself — every explanation, hint, and drill references the module content the learner is on. No generic answers; everything is grounded in this course.
