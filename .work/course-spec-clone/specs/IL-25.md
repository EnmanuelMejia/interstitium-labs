# IL-25 · IL-25 · Professional Cybersecurity Certificate Prep — Spec Sheet

## Identity

- title: IL-25 · Professional Cybersecurity Certificate Prep
- tagline: Think like an attacker. Defend like a professional.
- blurb: IL-25 is a professional cybersecurity certificate-prep track in three arcs: risk management (risk, foundations, federal role, threats, critical infrastructure, IAM, job prep), defensive operations (defensive intro, cryptography, SOC, incident response, secure sysadmin and netadmin, cloud security, job prep), and offensive cybersecurity (offensive intro, pentesting I & II, social engineering, AI in cybersecurity, policy/privacy/governance, OT/IoT risk, capstone). Three prereq-math modules embedded. Cross-linked to IL-29 (flagship). Independent prep for Security+ and CySA+.

## Learning Outcomes (objectives)
1. Manage cybersecurity risk: frameworks, assessment, and communication to leadership.
2. Explain the federal government's role in cybersecurity: law, operations, standards.
3. Analyze threats and vulnerabilities with structured prioritization.
4. Design and evaluate IAM: concepts, layers, and technology.
5. Operate defensively: SOC workflows, cryptography, incident response.
6. Administer systems and networks securely, including cloud.
7. Plan and scope penetration tests ethically and legally.
8. Recognize and defend against social engineering.
9. Apply AI in cybersecurity with honest understanding of its limits.
10. Navigate policy, privacy, regulation, data governance, and OT/IoT risk.

## Noah AI Integration (required)

> Noah AI is the core learning-experience driver for this course — not a bolt-on. Every module is taught with Noah as the Socratic tutor: learners work through the material in conversation with Noah, which is course-aware (grounded in this course's modules, notes, labs, quizzes, and drills), adapts with SM-2 spaced repetition, and speaks with 5 natural voices for a voice-first experience. Learners can talk with Noah via voice chat while working through modules — asking questions, working problems aloud, and getting challenged back before answers are given.

**Driver:** Noah drives the learning experience: guided Socratic walkthroughs of each module, Socratic questioning before answers, drill generation from the learner's weak spots, and lab debriefs that connect hands-on work back to the module's objectives.

**Personas → modules:**

- **Visionary** — Maps the career: from risk management to SOC to offensive — where each track leads. _(modules: IL25-M01, IL25-M08, IL25-M16, IL25-M24)_
- **Engineer** — Drills the technical core: crypto, IAM, networks, cloud — precise and procedural. _(modules: IL25-M06, IL25-M07, IL25-M10, IL25-M14, IL25-M15)_
- **Philosopher** — Interrogates the ethics: offensive work, disclosure, privacy — the responsibilities of capability. _(modules: IL25-M17, IL25-M20, IL25-M22)_
- **Mentor** — Prepares you for the job: SOC workflows, IR order-of-operations, certification readiness. _(modules: IL25-M09, IL25-M11, IL25-M12)_
- **Trickster** — Red-teams your defenses: the phishing email, the misconfigured firewall, the unpatched host — find it before it finds you. _(modules: IL25-M04, IL25-M14, IL25-M18, IL25-M19)_

**Voices:** 5 natural voices. Five natural voices. Voice-first: learners talk with Noah via voice chat while working through modules — hands free for labs, terminals, and notebooks.

**Adaptive:** SM-2 spaced repetition. Noah schedules flashcard and drill reviews with the SM-2 algorithm, prioritizing cards and modules the learner gets wrong. Forgetting curves are fought per-learner, not per-cohort.

**Course-aware:** Noah tutors from the course itself — every explanation, hint, and drill references the module content the learner is on. No generic answers; everything is grounded in this course.

**Course note:** Independent certification preparation aligned to publicly published exam objectives (CompTIA Security+, CySA+). Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any vendor; confers no certificate.
