## Learning Outcomes (objectives)
1. Run TPM interviews: program design, execution discipline, and cross-functional influence without authority.
2. Run EM interviews: people leadership, delivery ownership, hiring systems, and engineering strategy.
3. Run PM interviews: product sense, execution, metrics judgment, and tight behavioral storytelling.
4. Demonstrate technical PM depth: systems literacy, API/platform thinking, and data literacy with SQL.
5. Ship AI products with judgment: GenAI literacy, LLM product patterns, eval/UX design, and risk ownership.
6. Perform under interview conditions: timed mocks, rubrics, and iteration between rounds.
