  1. What is CIDR and why does /20 matter for a VNet plan?
  2. Explain Terraform state and why locking matters.
  3. Docker image layers — what invalidates the cache?
  4. How does Vault differ from secrets in env files?
  5. SLO vs alert?
  6. Blue/green vs rolling deploy tradeoff.
  7. First 15 minutes after a leaked cloud key.
