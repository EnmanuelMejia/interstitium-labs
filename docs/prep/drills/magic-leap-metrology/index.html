Honesty rules

  • Map transferable metrology / quality / ops-leadership experience without inventing Magic Leap tenure.
  • Interstitium Labs is portfolio craft — not a claim of AR optics fab employment.
  • Prefer measured STAR stories (SPC, MSA, team lead, RCA) over buzzwords.

Know the target

Magic Leap builds spatial computing / AR optics (waveguides, nanoimprint-related processes). Strategic context often cited in prep: production scale-up (e.g. Pegatron-style partnerships), microLED / XR ecosystem alliances, and heavy investment in usable FOV form factors. Values themes: innovation, unity, user-centric products.

Role shape (Senior Manager, Metrology & Optical Test): player-coach for metrology, calibration, and instrumentation — including nanostructure characterization and optical performance test — typically aligned to waveguide manufacturing. Three pillars:

  1. Strategy & leadership — metrology roadmap, grow the team, continuous improvement culture.
  2. Measurement systems — calibration/maintenance, ISO/IEC 17025 traceability, new characterization methods.
  3. Technical SME — hard measurement problems; partner with R&D, manufacturing, materials.

Optical KPIs to speak fluently: MTF, stray light, eyebox uniformity, color shift, efficiency vs. angle.

Technical question bank

Optical & grating metrology

  • Walk through hands-on experience with material / nanostructure characterization relevant to waveguides or precision optics.
  • How do you attack a complex dimensional or optical measurement problem in a cleanroom?
  • Which tools and methods would you prioritize for diffraction-grating / waveguide critical dimensions and optical KPIs (MTF, uniformity, efficiency)?

STAR tip: Situation = conflicting tester results; Action = MSA + method SOP + correlation study; Result = tighter Cp/Cpk or fewer escapes.

MSA · Gauge R&R · ANOVA

  • Purpose and methodology of a Gauge R&R (GR&R) study — what %R&R thresholds do you treat as acceptable vs. redo?
  • How would you use ANOVA to correlate / partition variance across optical testers or operators?
  • When is attribute vs. variable MSA the right call?
GR&R sketch:
- Select parts spanning process range
- Multiple operators × repeats (randomized)
- Decompose: repeatability (equipment) vs reproducibility (appraiser)
- Act: fixture, training, or method change — then re-study

Fab process & SPC

  • How does metrology feed process control (SPC charts, reaction plans) on a production line?
  • Example of reducing measurement-driven scrap or cycle time without weakening quality gates.
  • Supplier / partner lab correlation (e.g. internal lab vs. contract manufacturer).

ISO/IEC 17025 deep dive

Why it matters now: scaling production and supplier qualification demand traceable, comparable results between Magic Leap labs and partners.

  • How would you drive 17025-aligned practices (or accreditation) for new waveguide test methods?
  • Traceability chain: standards → reference → working instruments → uncertainty budget.
  • ISO 9001 vs 17025 — quality management vs. competence of testing/calibration labs.
  • Inter-laboratory comparisons: when two labs measure the same lot, how do you adjudicate bias?

Interview answer spine: competence of people + validated methods + calibrated equipment + uncertainty + impartiality + records.

Leadership & behavioral

  • Player-coach example: when you still did SME work while developing others.
  • Conflict between throughput pressure and measurement integrity — what did you protect and why?
  • Building a metrology roadmap for 12–24 months under capital constraints.
  • Cross-functional influence with R&D / Manufacturing without formal authority.
  • Blameless RCA after a metrology escape — controls you added (detective + preventive).

Use STAR. Quantify team size, cycle time, yield, or audit findings closed.

90-second pitch skeleton

1) Who you are (metrology / quality / ops leadership in one line)
2) Proof: one MSA or optical/measurement win with a number
3) Why Magic Leap now (scale + partner labs + optical KPIs)
4) How you’ll lead: roadmap, 17025 discipline, player-coach
5) Close: excited to help Magicleap’s next manufacturing chapter

Process tips

  • Re-read the live JD the night before — titles and org lines move.
  • Bring one diagram: uncertainty budget or GR&R flow you can whiteboard.
  • Prepare 3 questions: metrology org design, partner-lab correlation, capital priorities for new tools.
  • Optional ES fluency: you can offer bilingual communication; primary site content stays English.
