Honest scope

What this radar is — and what it isn't

This page aggregates a curated catalog of frontier tracks — the themes currently circulating through technical discourse at places like MIT's research channels, LinkedIn's professional feeds, and X's engineering corners — and joins each track to the Interstitium paths that actually teach it. That join is the whole trick: the radar is not a news feed. It's a routing table from what the industry is talking about to where you can learn it, with a deep link into Noah on every entry so you can start the Socratic turn on that exact topic.

Two things it honestly is not. First, it is not a live scrape of MIT, LinkedIn, or X — the catalog is curated and versioned on this site, so a track reflects what was judged significant, not what an algorithm amplified this morning. Second, it makes no predictions: "trending" means the topic has gravity in professional conversation right now, not that hiring managers will pay a premium for it next quarter. Your own scores and signals are stored locally in your browser, in your control, under your own lockup — never anyone else's skin.

How to use it

Scan the radar for a track you keep hearing about and don't fully own. Open its linked path — that path is the complete study sequence, from foundations to the interview — and when you want to pressure-test what you're reading, hand the track to Noah. Trend becomes path, path becomes practice, practice becomes readiness. That's the loop; the radar is just the entry point that starts it from the industry's vocabulary instead of the curriculum's.
