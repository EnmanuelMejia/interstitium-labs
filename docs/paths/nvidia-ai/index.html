Suggested 10-week learning plan

Sustainable pace: roughly 2–4 hours/week (longer weeks for the 8-hour labs). Adjust freely.

Phase 1 · Foundations (Weeks 1–3)

  • Week 1 — Generative AI Explained + Building A Brain in 10 Minutes
  • Week 2 — Agentic AI Explained + Beginner's Guide to Autonomous Robots
  • Week 3 — Review notes; skim the DLI free catalog for adjacent interests

Phase 2 · Applied skills (Weeks 4–7)

  • Week 4 — Data Science Workflows with Zero Code Changes (RAPIDS)
  • Week 5 — Generative AI in Digital Health
  • Week 6 — AI Infrastructure and Operations Fundamentals (split across the week if needed)
  • Week 7 — Developing with NVIDIA Omniverse

Phase 3 · Advanced development (Weeks 8–10)

  • Week 8 — Jetson Nano AI (part 1)
  • Week 9 — Jetson Nano AI (part 2) + first edge project artifact
  • Week 10 — Building RAG Agents with LLMs

DevOps / prep crossover

If you are on the BNY interview sprint or 12-mo DevSecOps mastery path, prioritize AI Infrastructure and Operations Fundamentals — GPU clusters, data-center ops, and platform literacy interviewers increasingly probe. This track does not replace Azure/K8s cert depth on the mastery page.
