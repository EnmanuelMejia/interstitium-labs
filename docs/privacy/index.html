Privacy

Your data, in plain language

Effective October 11, 2026. Interstitium Labs ("we", "the platform") runs interstitiumlabs.dev — courses, labs, and the Noah / Genesis AI tutors. This page says exactly what we collect, why, how long we keep it, and how you can get it or erase it. We wrote it to be read, not to hide behind. Where a law like the GDPR or CCPA gives you a right, this page tells you the button that exercises it.

What we collect

  • Account identity. Your email address, and — if you sign in with Google, GitHub, Microsoft, Apple, LinkedIn, Facebook, or X — your name and profile picture from that provider. Sign-in is passwordless (one-time email link or OAuth). We never see or store your password.
  • Learning records. Course and lab progress, assessment results, certificates you earn, and the notes, skill profiles, interview practice history, and preferences you choose to sync to your account.
  • Goals and jobs data. Goals you give Noah or Genesis (including money, health, shopping, and relationship goals) with their plans and progress; your jobs profile, application tracker, and saved job alerts.
  • Orders. What you bought, the amount, the payment provider, and its status. Card numbers never touch our servers — payments are processed by Stripe, PayPal, or NOWPayments. For direct crypto payments we record the order, the coin, and the transaction hash you submit.
  • AI conversations. Messages you send to Noah / Genesis are processed to produce replies (see "How AI processing works"). Voice input is transcribed to text to answer you.
  • Security logs. Sign-ins, sign-in failures, and sensitive account actions, with IP address, country, and browser type — used only to protect accounts.
  • Anonymous usage aggregates. Daily counts of page views, clicks, and errors, aggregated so they cannot be tied back to you. No advertising trackers, no selling data, no cross-site profiling.
  • On your device. The site stores data in your browser (local storage) so it works offline and remembers you on that device. If you never sign in, that data stays on your device and we cannot see it.

Why we use it

  • To run your account and sync your learning across your devices.
  • To teach: Noah / Genesis personalize tutoring using your progress, goals, and what you ask.
  • To fulfill purchases and issue certificates.
  • To keep accounts secure and the platform within its operating limits (rate limits and abuse prevention).
  • To understand, in aggregate, what to improve.

We do not sell personal data, share it for advertising, or use your notes, goals, or conversations to train AI models.

How AI processing works

Noah and Genesis are AI assistants, not people. When you chat, your message and relevant context (for example your synced goals and progress) are sent to cloud AI models operated by Cloudflare (Workers AI) to generate the reply. Speech is transcribed by an AI speech service. AI answers can be wrong — verify anything important. The animated tutor videos are AI-generated likenesses, created at the direction of the platform's founder and his family. Conversations with the tutors are not reviewed by humans in the ordinary course of operations.

Your rights — and the buttons

  • Access / export. Your dashboard exports the learner state on your device. For everything in your account, use Download full account export on the account page — one JSON file with your profile, progress, synced stores, goals, certificates, jobs data, learning records (xAPI statements), and orders.
  • Deletion. Delete my account on the account page erases your account, progress, synced data, goals, certificates, jobs data, and sessions. Two honest exceptions, also stated on that page: order records are kept as financial records with your email pseudonymized, and security-log entries expire on their own within 90 days.
  • Correction. Edit your synced notes, profile, and preferences in the product; they overwrite the stored copies. Certificates can be revoked from your account.
  • Objection / restriction / portability. The export is machine-readable JSON (portable by design). For anything else, email us and we will respond within 30 days.

Who processes data for us

Cloudflare (hosting, edge storage, AI models, security), Stripe and PayPal and NOWPayments (payments — only if you buy), MailChannels / Resend (sign-in and transactional email), Deepgram via Cloudflare (voice), and the sign-in provider you choose. Retrieved learning sources (for example Wikipedia) receive only the search query, never your identity.

Cookies

One essential cookie: your sign-in session (HttpOnly, secure, 24-hour idle / 7-day maximum). No advertising or third-party tracking cookies. Cloudflare's privacy-respecting analytics may set a beacon for the anonymous aggregates described above.

Children

The platform is built for adult learners and professionals and is not directed at children under 13. The tutors are named for the founder's children and their animated likenesses appear with his direction; that does not make the service a children's product.

Changes and contact

The short version: we collect what a tutoring platform needs to teach you and remember your progress, we never sell it, AI conversations are processed by cloud AI to answer you, and you can download everything or erase your account from the account page at any time.
