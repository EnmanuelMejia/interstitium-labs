About

The connective tissue of knowledge.

Interstitium Labs is a portfolio knowledge operating system (KOS) — academies, katas, adaptive math, and public evidence — built to hold what sits between disciplines. It is not a paid employer and not a video LMS. Motto: Scientia Omnia Vincit.
