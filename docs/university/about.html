The method

01 · Place

Map the fringe

A CAT-style diagnostic finds the inner edge of what you are ready to learn — in minutes, not weeks of placement tests.

02 · Fringe

Work the edge

Adaptive sequencing serves the lowest-mastery ready topic. Instruction → check → Socratic feedback → next.

03 · Gate

Prove it

Timed gates confirm mastery. Misses update the fringe automatically — the loop is honest about what stuck.

04 · Deepen

Go official

Deepens route to official vendor portals and references. Orchestrate, don't clone — never a photocopy of anyone's PDF.

Principles

  1. Work the gap. The interstitium is the network that holds other networks together. We study what sits between disciplines, organs, and systems — and we build there.
  2. Quiet instruments. Identity is a tool, not a costume. Marks stay small. Motion stays slow. Gold is a nucleus, never a fill. Cyan is a signal, never a wash.
  3. Knowledge, held. Research is useless if it cannot travel. The academy, the letter, the deck, and the site are one voice: precise, unhurried, complete.
