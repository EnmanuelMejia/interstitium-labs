Narrator: Narrator: The alchemical magnum opus moves through four stages — nigredo, the blackening of decomposition; albedo, the whitening of purification; citrinitas, the yellowing of awakening; and rubedo, the reddening of completion.

Narrator: Narrator: In nigredo, the materia prima putrefies and breaks down — psychologically, the confrontation with the shadow and the dissolution of the old self.

Narrator: Narrator: Rubedo completes the work — the red stone symbolizes the union of opposites, the integrated Self in Jung's reading of the tradition.
