## Concept

JOINs: INNER keeps matches, LEFT keeps all left rows, FULL keeps everything, and the ON clause is the relationship. Draw the Venn diagram, then write the query.

## Voiceover cues

- `[0:04–0:10]` INNER: matches only — the overlap
- `[0:10–0:16]` LEFT: all left rows — NULLs where nothing matched
- `[0:16–0:22]` FULL: everything — both sides, NULLs included
- `[0:22–0:26]` ON is the relationship — WHERE is the filter
