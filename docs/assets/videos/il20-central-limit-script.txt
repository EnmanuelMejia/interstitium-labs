Narrator: Narrator: Take samples from any distribution — even a skewed one — and plot the means of many samples. Something remarkable happens.

Narrator: Narrator: As sample size grows, the distribution of sample means converges to a normal curve — regardless of the population's shape. That is the central limit theorem.

Narrator: Narrator: This is why confidence intervals and hypothesis tests work — the theorem guarantees the sampling distribution we need for statistical inference.
