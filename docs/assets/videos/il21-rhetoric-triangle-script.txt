# Narration script: The Rhetorical Triangle
# Style: story
# STATUS: Preview — narration coming (TTS blocked)

Narrator: Narrator: Aristotle's rhetorical triangle — ethos, the speaker's credibility; pathos, the audience's emotion; logos, the argument's logic. Persuasion needs all three.

Narrator: Narrator: Pure pathos manipulates, pure logos bores, pure ethos preaches. The triangle holds only when all three appeals bear weight.

Narrator: Narrator: Watch any great keynote — personal story for pathos, credentials for ethos, data for logos — the triangle is the hidden structure of persuasion.
