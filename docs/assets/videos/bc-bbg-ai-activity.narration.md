## Concept

The activity lifecycle: onCreate builds, onStart shows, onResume interacts, onPause/onStop retreat, onDestroy cleans. The OS owns your activity — the callbacks are your lease terms.

## Voiceover cues

- `[0:04–0:10]` onCreate: build it — once per birth
- `[0:10–0:16]` onStart/onResume: visible, then interactive
- `[0:16–0:22]` onPause/onStop: retreat gracefully — save state
- `[0:22–0:26]` onDestroy: clean up — the lease ends
