Narrator: Narrator: Ringfencing wraps an application in a policy boundary — the app can do its job inside the fence, but suspicious actions at the boundary are blocked.

Narrator: Narrator: A browser exploit might achieve code execution — but when the payload tries to spawn PowerShell or touch the registry, the ringfence kills it. The exploit is contained.

Narrator: Narrator: Policies distinguish legitimate child processes from malicious ones by behavior — Word spawning a printer driver is fine; Word spawning cmd.exe is not.
