## Concept

Distributions: normal for the well-behaved, binomial for yes/no trials, Poisson for rare events. Know the shape before the test — the wrong distribution is a wrong answer with confidence.

## Voiceover cues

- `[0:04–0:10]` Normal: the bell — means and errors
- `[0:10–0:16]` Binomial: yes/no trials — counted successes
- `[0:16–0:22]` Poisson: rare events — arrivals per hour
- `[0:22–0:26]` Match shape to question — or the p-value lies
