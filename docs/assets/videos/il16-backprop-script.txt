Narrator: Narrator: Backpropagation applies the chain rule in reverse — the loss gradient splits at each neuron, flowing back to every weight proportional to its contribution.

Narrator: Narrator: Each weight steps against its gradient scaled by the learning rate. Repeat over epochs and the loss descends — the network learns.
