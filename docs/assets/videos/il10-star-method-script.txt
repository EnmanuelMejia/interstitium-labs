Narrator: Narrator: STAR structures behavioral answers — Situation sets context, Task defines your responsibility, Action details what you did, Result quantifies the outcome.

Narrator: Narrator: Without structure, answers ramble. STAR compresses a two-minute story into a clear arc the interviewer can actually evaluate.

Narrator: Narrator: The Result carries the weight — quantified impact in percentages, revenue, or time saved is what interviewers remember and score.
