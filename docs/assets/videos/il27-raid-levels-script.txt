Narrator: Narrator: RAID zero stripes data across disks — blazing fast, full capacity — but one disk failure destroys the array. Speed with zero safety net.

Narrator: Narrator: RAID one mirrors everything — half the capacity, survives any single failure. RAID five stripes parity instead, surviving one failure with less overhead.

Narrator: Narrator: RAID six survives two failures with dual parity; RAID ten mirrors then stripes for performance plus redundancy. Match the level to the failure tolerance you need.
