Narrator: Narrator: Hand-patched servers become snowflakes — unique, undocumented, unreproducible. Immutable infrastructure replaces them with versioned images built from code.

Narrator: Narrator: Every deploy builds a fresh image, tests it, and swaps it in — old instances are destroyed, never modified. What runs is exactly what was tested.

Narrator: Narrator: Rollback stops being scary — it is redeploying the previous known-good image. Recovery time drops from hours of debugging to minutes of redeploy.
